Privacy policy and cookies

Last updated: 2026-10-19

Who we are

RiftBuild is a free, unofficial app (Android and web) that suggests Wild Rift builds. It has no accounts, no server of its own and no user database.

What is stored on your phone

Your settings, favourite and recent champions, match history, timers and game data are stored only in the app's private storage. Your AI key, if you add one, is stored encrypted with the Android Keystore. None of it is copied to the cloud (Android backup is disabled). Uninstalling the app deletes everything. In the web version the same data is kept in your browser's local storage (the AI key unencrypted) and is erased when you clear the site data.

Cookies

The app uses no cookies and no advertising trackers. It only uses the device's local storage to remember your settings, which is why there is no cookie banner.

Who data is shared with

Only if you set it up. (1) If you turn on AI, the champion, lane and enemies/allies you pick are sent to the provider you chose (OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI or Groq) together with your key; their own policies and your account costs apply. (2) The app downloads game data from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com); GitHub can see your IP address as with any web download. We never send your key anywhere else.

Anonymous statistics (optional)

If you accept, the app sends anonymous usage counts (for example "app opened" or "build generated") to GoatCounter, a cookie-free service. No champions, builds, keys or text are sent and no profile of you is created. You can turn this on or off any time in Settings. If you do not accept, nothing is sent.

Android permissions

Display over other apps: to draw the floating bubble with your build. Notification: Android requires one while the bubble is active. Internet: for game data and, if you enable it, AI. Vibration: timer alerts.

Your rights

Since we keep no data about you on servers, you can erase everything by uninstalling the app. For privacy questions, use the contact below.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, it will be updated here and in the app, with the date above.
